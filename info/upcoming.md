# 🚀 Upcoming Features & Roadmap

## 🗓️ Release Timeline
Many users are asking, "When will LiveLens be available?". Here is our current timeline:

- **Release Date:** Currently, there is **no fixed release date** for either the Android or iOS versions. We are working hard to ensure the app is stable and feature-rich before the official launch.
- **Platform Priority:** The **Android version will be released first**. It is our primary platform and where development is most advanced.
- **iOS Following Android:** The iOS version will only be developed and released **after** the Android version is fully polished, released on the Play Store, and has received sufficient community support and funding. Building for iOS requires specialized hardware (Mac and iPhone) and additional resources, which is why it is planned as a secondary phase.
