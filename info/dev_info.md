# Developer & Studio Information

## Meet the Developer

LiveLens is developed and led by **Arpan Das**, an independent creator and streamer based in **Kolkata, West Bengal, India**. 

The project is a labor of love, built independently by Arpan at night around his full-time job. The app was born out of Arpan's firsthand experience as a mobile streamer. Tired of the technical compromises—such as bitrate restrictions, weak overlay support, forced watermarks, and hidden paywalls—he decided to build a "serious production surface" for Android.

### The Learning Curve & Journey
Arpan started this project with almost no prior streaming-engine knowledge. He spent several years self-teaching the complexities of:
- **OpenGL ES Pipelines**: For high-performance mobile rendering.
- **MediaCodec Encoding**: Utilizing FBOs (Frame Buffer Objects) and PBuffers for professional-grade video.
- **Low-Latency Audio**: To ensure perfect sync during broadcasts.

> **"Freedom in your pocket, not a locked-down tool."**

The journey wasn't easy. At one point, the technical scope became so overwhelming that it led to a significant "break point" and burnout, causing a pause in development for several months. However, the passion for mobile creation brought him back, solving one rendering and audio issue at a time until LiveLens became what it is today.

## 🎨 Bycon Studio

LiveLens is the flagship project of **Bycon Studio**, an independent, creator-owned entity focused on building tools that prioritize user freedom and privacy. The studio's mission is to provide PC-style control to Android creators without forced account requirements or data harvesting.

### The LiveLens Ecosystem
- **LiveLens (Main)**: The full-featured streaming and recording powerhouse.
- **LiveLens Lite**: A companion tool designed for low-latency voice chat, perfect for coordinating with teams without heavy background overhead.

### Our Philosophy
- **No Ads, No Watermarks**: Your content belongs to you. We believe the app should never interfere with your broadcast.
- **Privacy First**: No forced account creation and no data harvesting.
- **Empty Canvas Workflow**: We provide the tools; you provide the creativity. We don't force you into predefined templates.
- **Community Funded**: Instead of aggressive monetization, LiveLens relies on community support to remain sustainable.
