# Support & Contribute to LiveLens

## Can I contribute to LiveLens?

**Yes, absolutely!** 

LiveLens is an independent, solo developer project. Community support is what allows the app to remain **ad-free, watermark-free, and subscription-free**. Every contribution, whether financial or through feedback, directly helps in building the future of mobile streaming.

---

## 🚀 Ways to Support Financially

If you find LiveLens valuable for your streaming journey and would like to help keep the development active, you can support through the following channels:

### 1. GitHub Sponsors
Become a monthly or one-time sponsor on GitHub. This is one of the best ways to support the project's long-term sustainability.
👉 **[Support on GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/ByconStudio)**

### 2. Official Support Page
Our website features various local and international donation methods, including **UPI (India)** and **PayPal**.
👉 **[View all Support Options](https://www.livelens.live/pages/support.html)**

> **Note**: Contributions are entirely optional. Supporting the project does not unlock "exclusive" features; LiveLens aims to keep its core professional tools accessible to all creators regardless of their ability to donate.

---

## 🛠️ Non-Financial Contributions

You don't need to spend money to help LiveLens grow! Here are other vital ways to contribute:

- **Report Bugs**: If something isn't working, let us know via [Discord](https://discord.gg/kUqmbKhs6w) or Email.
- **Suggest Features**: Have an idea to make streaming better? Share it with the community!
- **Spread the Word**: Recommend LiveLens to fellow streamers. Your word-of-mouth support helps us reach more creators.
- **Join the Community**: Be part of our Discord server to help other users and share your streaming setups.

---

## 💎 Why your support matters

LiveLens is built "at night" around a full-time commitment. Your support helps cover:
- Server and backend costs for collaborative features.
- Hardware for testing (new phones, capture cards).
- The immense amount of time required to develop a high-performance streaming engine from scratch.

Thank you for being part of the Bycon Studio journey!
