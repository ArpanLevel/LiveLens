# Music System & Background Tracks

## Overview

LiveLens features a built-in background music system that allows you to play copyright-free music or your own local tracks during different parts of your stream. You can configure separate tracks for your intro, your breaks, and your main gameplay.

---

## 🎵 Music Configuration

### Master Switch
Enable the **Music System** in settings to reveal individual track controls. 
> **Important**: Upon first activation, you must read and accept the Music Disclaimer regarding copyright and content usage.

### 1. BRB Music
- Plays only when the **BRB (Be Right Back)** asset is active on your stream.
- Supports individual volume control and infinite looping.

### 2. Starting Soon Music
- Plays while the **Starting Soon** intro screen is active.
- Helps set the mood for your stream before you begin talking.

### 3. Stream Music (Gameplay/Background)
- Plays continuously during your main stream.
- **Flexible Sources**: You can pick a **Single File** or an **Entire Folder**.
- **Playlist Features**: If a folder is selected, you can enable **Shuffle Playlist** to randomize the tracks.
- **Repeat Track**: Toggle this to loop a single favorite song.

---

## 🎧 Global Music Controls

- **Monitoring (Hear it)**: Toggle this to listen to the background music yourself through your headphones. If disabled, the music will only be audible to your viewers.
- **Auto-Stop**: When enabled, the music system will automatically stop playing as soon as you end your stream or recording.
- **Individual Volumes**: Each section (BRB, Soon, Stream) has its own independent volume slider (0% to 100%) so you can balance music against your voice.

---

## Music Settings Summary

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Folder Support | Play all music files from a specific directory |
| Shuffle Mode | Randomize playback for folder-based playlists |
| Asset Sync | Automatically switches tracks based on BRB/Soon status |
| Monitoring | Listen to the stream music locally |
| Disclaimer | Required legal agreement for music usage |
