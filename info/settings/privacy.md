# Privacy & Permissions

## Overview

LiveLens is built with a **Privacy-First** philosophy. We believe that your data and your content belong solely to you. The app is designed to work mostly offline for its core rendering tasks, ensuring maximum security for creators.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy Policy

- **No Data Harvesting**: LiveLens does not track your location, read your contacts, or sell your usage habits to third parties.
- **Local Processing**: All scene rendering (Cameras, VTubers, Overlays) happens entirely on your device.
- **No Forced Accounts**: You can use the app's professional production tools and local recording features without ever creating a LiveLens account.
- **Secure Platform Login**: When you link YouTube or Twitch, LiveLens uses official **OAuth** protocols. We never see or store your platform passwords.

---

## 🔑 Required Permissions

To provide a professional streaming experience, LiveLens requires the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To add your face or environment to the stream. |
| **Microphone** | To capture your voice and drive VTuber lip-syncing. |
| **Storage (Files)** | To load your images, videos, fonts, and VTuber models. |
| **Overlay** | To show the Control Bubble and Chat over your games. |
| **Screen Capture** | To stream your gameplay or app demonstrations. |
| **Notifications** | To show stream status and controls in your system tray. |

---

## 🗑️ Data Deletion

If you decide to stop using LiveLens, you have full control over your data:
- **Local Data**: Simply uninstalling the app removes all locally stored scene configurations and preferences.
- **Platform Links**: You can log out of YouTube/Twitch at any time from the **Account Settings** to revoke access.

---

## 📩 Support & Feedback

- **Email**: [support@livelens.live](mailto:support@livelens.live)
